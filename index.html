Hollowbrook Pool & Spa was started in 1987 by Gary and Donna Hollowbrook out of their garage on Elm Street. 20+ years later we have a 4,000 sq ft showroom, 3 service trucks and a staff of 11 (plus our shop dog, Biscuit!).
